FAQ

Common questions

Which course is best after Plus Two commerce?

There is no single best route for everyone. CA, ACCA, CMA, integrated degree programmes and practical programmes suit different goals. Admissions can compare options based on your interests, time commitment and career direction.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Logic’s current batch information includes online, offline and hybrid learning modes, depending on the programme and intake.

What is the fee structure?

Fees can change by programme, batch, location and offer period. Use the enquiry form for the current approved fee quote instead of relying on an old website figure.

How do I find the next batch?

Use the Batch Schedule page. Published dates can change, so confirm seat availability with admissions before making travel or payment plans.

How does placement support work?

Logic publicly promotes placement assistance and reports 8,000+ placements. Exact eligibility, opportunities and recruitment timelines depend on the programme and employer requirements.
