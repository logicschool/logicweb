What to Know Before You Start ACCA

Logic Career Guide • Updated for the current website edition

Think globally, plan locally

ACCA is commonly explored by students interested in international accounting and finance. Your study plan should still be built around your present academic stage, exemptions, available time and long-term location goals.

Understand your exemption position

Integrated degree students and graduates may be eligible for exemptions depending on their qualification. Confirm your exact exemption status before planning a completion timeline.

Build consistency into the timetable

Professional exams reward regular concept practice, question practice and review. A sustainable weekly routine is more useful than short bursts of high-intensity study.

Add practical workplace skills

Communication, Excel, reporting tools and practical accounting exposure can complement professional study and help students prepare for internships and entry-level roles.
