Choosing a Commerce Career Path After Plus Two

Logic Career Guide • Updated for the current website edition

Do not begin with salary claims

Start with your interests, aptitude, study style and the kind of work you want to learn. Salary varies widely by role, experience, employer, location and performance.

Compare degree and professional routes

Some students prefer a traditional degree first, while others choose integrated pathways that combine a degree with a professional qualification. Compare workload, recognition, cost and flexibility.

Ask where the qualification is strongest

Indian professional qualifications and international qualifications can lead to different role ecosystems. Neither is automatically better; the fit depends on your goals.

Use a structured assessment

Write down your preferred exam style, career location, budget, earning timeline and need for international recognition. A career assessment can turn those preferences into a clearer shortlist.
