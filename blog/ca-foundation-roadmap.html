CA Foundation: Build a Preparation System That Lasts

Logic Career Guide • Updated for the current website edition

Create a subject rotation

Instead of spending entire weeks on one subject, use a repeatable rotation so every subject receives concept learning, practice and revision.

Track mistakes, not only marks

Mock tests are useful when you review why marks were lost. Keep a short error log for weak concepts, presentation issues and time-management mistakes.

Plan backward from the exam window

Divide the remaining time into learning, first revision, question practice and final revision. Keep buffer days for difficult chapters and unexpected delays.

Protect consistency

A realistic timetable that you can repeat is stronger than an aggressive plan that collapses after a few days. Sleep, breaks and regular testing are part of the preparation system.
