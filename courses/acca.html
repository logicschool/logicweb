Global pathway

A professional qualification designed for accounting and finance careers across markets.

Up to 13 exams

Progressive structure

Advance from core knowledge to applied skills and strategic professional capability.

Career breadth

Reporting, audit, tax, finance, performance and advisory roles.

COURSE ROUTE

Your progression, step by step.

The exact registration, eligibility, exemption and assessment rules can change. Use this as a learning roadmap and confirm current terms with admissions / the relevant body.

01

Applied Knowledge

Build essential business, management accounting and financial accounting knowledge.

02

Applied Skills

Develop law, tax, reporting, audit, performance and financial-management capability.

03

Strategic Professional

Apply advanced judgement across strategic business, reporting and specialist options.

04

Ethics & Experience

Complete the relevant ethics and practical-experience requirements for membership progression.

LEARNING FOCUS

What you will build depth in.

Instead of a long wall of syllabus text, the core learning areas are grouped into practical themes.

Current papers, modules, exemptions and exam rules should always be verified for the intake you are joining.

Financial Reporting

Prepare, interpret and evaluate financial information.

Audit & Assurance

Understand assurance engagements, controls and audit evidence.

Performance & Finance

Measure performance and make finance decisions.

Strategy & Leadership

Integrate business, risk, governance and strategic thinking.
