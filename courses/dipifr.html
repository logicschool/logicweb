COURSE ROUTE

Your progression, step by step.

The exact registration, eligibility, exemption and assessment rules can change. Use this as a learning roadmap and confirm current terms with admissions / the relevant body.

01

IFRS Framework

Understand the conceptual basis and presentation principles behind international reporting.

02

Core Standards

Study major IFRS/IAS topics and their practical accounting treatment.

03

Group Reporting

Develop consolidation and group-financial-statement capability.

04

Exam Preparation

Practise scenario-based application and time-managed exam technique.

Financial Statements

Group Accounts

Scenario Practice
