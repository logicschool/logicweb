Privacy Policy

A practical privacy page for the website enquiry forms included in this build.

Information collected

Website forms may collect name, phone number, email address, programme interest, location preference and message content when voluntarily submitted.

How it is used

Submitted information is intended for admissions, counselling, support and related communication. This project’s included Node server stores form submissions locally in data/leads.ndjson unless you connect your own CRM endpoint.

Your deployment responsibilities

Before production launch, connect the forms to Logic’s approved CRM/database, define retention and access controls, and ensure this policy matches the organisation’s actual data practices. The official Logic privacy policy should remain the governing reference.
